# Source register

This register identifies the primary versions underlying the [related-work comparison](related-work.md). Page numbers refer to the linked PDF, counting its first page as page 1. Published journal references identify the work; a comparison based on a particular preprint version is labeled as such. Inspection of selected passages is not a claim that every proof in a source was independently verified. Sources were checked on 11 November 2026.

The matching machine-readable bibliography is [bibliography.bib](bibliography.bib). Its citation keys match the identifiers below.

## Collective decay and quantum memories

### LW03 — General decoherence-free subspace conditions

Daniel A. Lidar and K. Birgitta Whaley, **Decoherence-Free Subspaces and Subsystems**, in *Irreversible Quantum Dynamics*, Lecture Notes in Physics **622**, 83–120 (2003). [arXiv:quant-ph/0301032v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0301032v1); [versioned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0301032v1).

- **Inspected:** Section 5.1, Eq. (9) and Theorem 1, pp. 9–10; Section 5.3, Eq. (16) and Theorem 4, p. 12; Section 5.6, p. 16.
- **Attribution:** standard DFS conditions, the separate system-Hamiltonian invariance requirement, and the distinction between Hamiltonian and Lindblad formulations.
- **Comparison boundary:** the repository's microscopic protection is conditioned on an initially vacuum bath; it does not claim protection for every bath state.

### DG98 — Optimal collective amplitude-damping codes

Lu-Ming Duan and Guang-Can Guo, **Optimal quantum codes for preventing collective amplitude damping**, *Physical Review A* **58**, 3491–3495 (1998). [Published DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.58.3491); [arXiv:quant-ph/9804014v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9804014v1); [versioned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/9804014v1).

- **Inspected:** dimension statement, p. 3; collective-spin decomposition and encoding efficiency, pp. 6–9; examples, pp. 9–10.
- **Attribution:** exact uniform rank-one dark dimension and asymptotically unit-rate encoding.
- **Comparison boundary:** one collective decay profile; the growing-rank arbitrary-profile guarantee is not this source's statement.

### TNG25 — Inhomogeneous rank-one dark-state counting

L. Theerthagiri, Rajesh Narayanan and R. Ganesh, **Measuring dark state number in the Tavis-Cummings model**, [arXiv:2509.14313v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14313v1), submitted 17 September 2025. [Versioned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.14313v1).

- **Inspected:** Sections II–V, pp. 2–3; rank-nullity and nonzero-coupling qualification, p. 3; Appendix B, pp. 5–6.
- **Attribution:** for a single arbitrary nonzero profile, the excitation-$`s`$ dark dimension is $`\max\{\binom Ns-\binom N{s-1},0\}`$.
- **Comparison boundary:** coupling-disorder independence for one profile does not supply simultaneous-kernel bounds for several independent profiles.

### Emary13 — Multimode cavity dark states at weak excitation

Clive Emary, **Dark states in multi-mode multi-atom Jaynes-Cummings systems**, *Journal of Physics B: Atomic, Molecular and Optical Physics* **46**, 224008 (2013). [Published DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/0953-4075/46/22/224008); [arXiv:1304.6240v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.6240v1); [versioned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1304.6240v1).

- **Inspected:** p. 2, “Weak excitation model,” Eqs. (7)–(12); multimode discussion and conclusion, pp. 4–5.
- **Attribution:** arbitrary coupling-matrix singular-value decomposition and multimode bright/dark structure.
- **Comparison boundary:** the model restricts the relevant basis to vacuum and single-excitation states.

### Lee26 — Multiple collective jumps with partial permutation symmetry

Joseph T. Lee, Silvia Cardenas-Lopez, Stuart J. Masson, Rahul Trivedi and Ana Asenjo-Garcia, **Exact many-body quantum dynamics in one-dimensional baths via collective spins**, *Physical Review A* **113**, L061701 (2026). [Published DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/zcjd-jlpj); [arXiv:2505.00588v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.00588v2), revised 12 June 2026; [versioned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.00588v2).

- **Inspected:** collective-jump normal form and algebra, p. 3, Eq. (7); Hamiltonian caveat, Fig. 2 caption; dark-state discussion, pp. 4–5; supplemental algebra and dark constructions, pp. 12 and 15–17.
- **Attribution:** exact collective-spin reductions and many-excitation dark states for commensurate coupling patterns.
- **Comparison boundary:** this symmetry-based reduction is not a profile-independent rank–redundancy theorem.

### HJM26 — Structured null spaces in hybrid systems

Catalin-Mihai Halati, Tony Jin and Roderich Moessner, **Constructing Large and Structured Decoherence-Free Subspaces in Hybrid Quantum Systems**, [arXiv:2610.12348v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12348v2). [Versioned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.12348v2).

- **Inspected:** abstract and model overview; matter-null-space construction; Section IV.A, p. 8, and IV.B, pp. 9–10, including the collective spin-$`1/2`$ dimension.
- **Attribution:** lossy-boson selection of matter null spaces and representation-theoretic constructions with large dark spaces.
- **Comparison boundary:** the inspected construction uses a single collective matter operator, whereas the present bound controls intersections for several independent inhomogeneous profiles.

### Han26 — Inhomogeneous coupling and optical echo memories

Fumiya Hanamura, Sicheng Bao, Jie Lerk Yoo, Alexia Auffèves and Steven Touzard, **Inhomogeneous Light-Matter Coupling as a Resource for Noiseless Quantum Memories**, [arXiv:2605.26783v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26783v4), revised 24 August 2026. [Versioned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.26783v4); [versioned HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.26783v4).

- **Inspected:** Sections II–III and Fig. 1, pp. 2–3; Appendix A.2 derivations near both polarized states; Appendix H, pp. 33–35, especially Eq. (H4).
- **Attribution:** bright/dark input–output description and coupling-dependent echo silencing.
- **Comparison boundary:** one physical resonator and a Holstein–Primakoff approximation; this is not an exact arbitrary-rank register-capacity theorem.

### BPC08 — Hamiltonian-induced leakage from a dark space

Peter G. Brooke, Manas K. Patra and James D. Cresser, **Decoherence-free quantum information in the presence of dynamical evolution**, *Physical Review A* **77**, 062313 (2008). [Published DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.77.062313); [arXiv:0806.3861v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.3861v1); [versioned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0806.3861v1).

- **Inspected:** abstract and Introduction, p. 1, explaining the distinction between dissipative darkness and invariance under bath-induced Hamiltonians.
- **Attribution:** the physical caveat that coherent interactions can take encoded states out of a dissipative dark space.
- **Comparison boundary:** the present full vacuum-cavity invariance argument imposes an explicit compatible Hamiltonian; it makes no generic free-space claim.

### AG17 — Subradiance and selective radiance for photon storage

A. Asenjo-Garcia, M. Moreno-Cardoner, A. Albrecht, H. J. Kimble and D. E. Chang, **Exponential Improvement in Photon Storage Fidelities Using Subradiance and “Selective Radiance” in Atomic Arrays**, *Physical Review X* **7**, 031024 (2017). [Published DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.7.031024); [arXiv:1703.03382v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.03382v2); [versioned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1703.03382v2).

- **Inspected:** published abstract and preprint Introduction, pp. 1–2, for the stated ordered-array and photon-interface result. The storage-protocol proof was not independently audited.
- **Attribution:** collective suppression of unwanted emission and its use in high-fidelity optical memories, including many-excitation physics.
- **Comparison boundary:** improved photon-interface error is a different performance variable from the dimension of a universal passive register code.

## Algebraic ingredients

### Oud09 — Finite local-algebra duality

Rémy Oudompheng, **Duality and canonical modules**, Spring School in Local Algebra, 1 April 2009. [Author-hosted notes](https://www.normalesup.org/~oudomphe/textes/alg-20090401.pdf).

- **Inspected:** Section 2.2, Proposition 9 and Theorem 10, pp. 3–4; definitions of socle and injective hull on p. 3.
- **Attribution:** the duality between a module's socle and its dual's top, and the finite-dimensional dualizing module $`\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb C}(A,\mathbb C)`$.
- **Use here:** these standard facts imply $`\dim M\leq\dim A\,\dim\operatorname{soc}M`$. The notes are an accessible exposition, not an assertion of historical priority for that elementary inequality.

### BL26 — Powers of general linear forms

Mats Boij and Samuel Lundqvist, **On the Hilbert series of ideals generated by powers of general linear forms**, [arXiv:2608.22823v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22823v1), submitted 24 August 2026. [Versioned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.22823v1). The PDF title includes “powers of”; the abstract landing-page title omits those words.

- **Inspected:** Sections 1–2; inverse-system equality (2.1), p. 3; Theorem 3.1, p. 6, for $`n+2`$ powers of general linear forms in $`n`$ variables; Sections 3–4's scope and exceptions.
- **Attribution:** the exact generic Hilbert-series result relevant to rank two after eliminating two linear constraints.
- **Comparison boundary:** generic sector nullities are the all-profile minima by polynomial-matrix rank semicontinuity. Where the specialization's hypotheses apply, the generic rank-two formula therefore also supplies sharper uniform lower bounds than the elementary estimate used here. No unproved generic Hilbert-series formula is assumed in this repository.

Christopher M. Dawson and Michael A. Nielsen, **The Solovay-Kitaev algorithm**, [arXiv:quant-ph/0505030v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0505030v2), revised 23 August 2005. [Versioned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0505030v2).

- **Checked for this register:** primary version metadata and abstract; no new passage-level inspection is asserted here.
- **Attribution:** efficient classical compilation of single-qubit gates into a fixed finite universal set, with polylogarithmic precision overhead. This compilation ingredient is inherited.

## Reading and provenance limits

The references above distinguish a source's theorem from an interpretation obtained by translating it into the present notation. In particular, the squarefree-quotient identification and the substitution $`n=N-2`$ in [BL26] are explicit mathematical translations. Selected-passage comparisons do not establish an exhaustive priority claim. The core results must stand on the proofs supplied in this repository.
