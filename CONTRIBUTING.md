# Maintaining the scientific account

The authoritative definitions and physical interface live in `science/model.md`; the finite dimension theorem and worst-profile dark-code law live in `science/rank-law.md`; endpoint complexity and precision live in `science/encoding-and-precision.md`. Update the overview, worked examples, limitations and source comparison whenever their premises change.

Keep common-kernel redundancy distinct from unrestricted passive capacity, supplied exact coefficients distinct from calibration, and normalized-Choi diagnostics distinct from diamond-norm upper bounds. Preserve the existing MIT license and accurate primary-source versions. Publication planning and personal learning records do not belong in the scientific account.

Run the affected commands in [verification/README.md](verification/README.md). Inspect mathematical content in its actual hosting renderer before claiming presentation readiness; local parsing and numerical checks do not establish visual correctness. Use branches and reviewable pull requests, and inspect the current default branch before integrating concurrent work.
